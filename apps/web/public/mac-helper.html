Two local workflows

One helper. Two ways out.

Editable export

Freeform → Excalidraw

  1. Copy your selection in Freeform.
  2. Save it with the capture command.
  3. Open it in Capture Tester.
./boardeject-capture Board.boardeject
Open Capture Tester →
Local backup

Freeform → verified archive

  1. Scan your local Freeform boards.
  2. Choose one board.
  3. Create and verify its archive.
./boardeject-mac scan
./boardeject-mac create BOARD_UUID Board.boardejectarchive
./boardeject-mac verify Board.boardejectarchive
