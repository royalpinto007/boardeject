First open: approve the helper

Approve the unsigned helper once.

BoardEject is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized. macOS blocks the first launch until you approve it.

  1. Open the DMG and drag BoardEject Helper to Applications.
  2. Open the app once and dismiss the warning.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway.
  5. Authenticate, then confirm Open.

macOS remembers this exception for later launches. Only approve the helper downloaded from this page. These steps follow Apple's Gatekeeper guidance ↗.
