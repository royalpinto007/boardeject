Public samples for trying the capture tester.

Where they came from

The samples were copied unchanged from the fixtures directory of can1357/libfreeform ↗ at commit f35764612125ea8385239990ce4f3655b0f4297f.

What they prove

The ink sample demonstrates editable parser output. It does not prove Freeform pressure fidelity. The board sample intentionally exercises the safe version-7 rejection report. Neither sample is a claim of complete Freeform compatibility.
