# BoardEject v0.0.2

BoardEject converts supported Apple Freeform clipboard content into editable
Excalidraw files instead of flattened PDF or image exports.

This release adds genuine Freeform-backed table conversion for unequal rows and
columns, insert/delete/reorder operations, multiple editable tables, colors,
borders, and attached text. It also adds verified image mask and shadow handling,
improves mixed and multiline editable text, preserves tested nested transform and
connector evidence, and fixes masked-ink handling. The suite contains 125 tests.

Freeform 4.5 on macOS now has documented Pen behavior: it emits vector shape
records in the tested flow, not PencilKit drawing payloads, and exposes no eraser.
iPad-originated Apple Pencil pressure and eraser round-trip validation remains
future work in [issue #20](https://github.com/royalpinto007/boardeject/issues/20).
Other unsupported elements remain visible in conversion reports.

Try it at [boardeject.dev](https://boardeject.dev), or inspect a capture at
[boardeject.dev/test-capture](https://boardeject.dev/test-capture). Conversion is
local-first: boards are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or stored
by BoardEject.

This is an early, limited-scope release. Keep your original board, review the
[support matrix](https://github.com/royalpinto007/boardeject#support-matrix), and
help extend fixture-backed fidelity through the open issues.
